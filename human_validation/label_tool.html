Inside: any part of it overlaps the footprint.
Person: any person, near or far, even if not in the way.
Other object: a separate object of any size, near or far, even if it is not in the way (furniture, bag, bin, pole, sign, vehicle…). Walls, floor, door frames and other building structure don't count.
Clear: neither of the above.
Both a person and an object: label it Person. Person takes priority, the same rule the auto-labeler uses.
Mark what is there first. Whether it is in the way is the separate question below.
Blocks path: would someone walking this way have to stop or steer around something, including walls or closed doors?
